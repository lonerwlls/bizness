Seven a.m. Your phone buzzes before your alarm. "In ten seconds, Mom yells breakfast." Ten seconds later: "Breakfast!" Your phone just predicted the future. And it's going to be so much weirder than you think.
Rule one: it's never wrong. "Don't step left." You step left. Gum. On your brand new sneakers.
Rule two: it spoils everything. Movie night: "The dog did it." Your birthday: "It's a bike." You still act surprised.
Rule three: it makes you a legend. "Pop quiz. Question four. Answer B." You raise your hand before the teacher even asks. The whole class stares.
And the last rule: it knows who likes you. One new message: "She's going to say hi in three, two, one..." "Hi."
Next morning. Seven a.m. Your phone buzzes before your alarm. "In ten seconds, Mom yells breakfast." So this time, you yell it first.
