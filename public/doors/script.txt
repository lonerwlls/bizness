Monday morning. You open your bedroom door, and you step onto a beach. Sand. Waves. Seagulls. Every door now leads to another universe. And it's going to be so much weirder than you think.
Rule one: you never know where a door goes. The bathroom door? A snowy mountain. The fridge? Somehow, a gym.
Rule two: getting to school is a gamble. First door: a desert. Second door: a library. Third door: your school. On a Saturday.
Rule three: some doors are better than others. The janitor's closet leads to a pizza party. You tell nobody.
And the last rule: one door always leads home. Your front door. Mom is making pancakes.
Monday morning. You open your bedroom door, and you step onto the beach again. This time, you brought sunscreen.
