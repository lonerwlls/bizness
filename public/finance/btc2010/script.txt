What if you put just one dollar into Bitcoin in early 2010? Back then, a bitcoin cost less than a cent. Your one dollar buys about three hundred coins. You forget about them. May 2010. A guy pays ten thousand bitcoins for two pizzas. 2011. Bitcoin hits thirty dollars. Your dollar is now worth almost ten thousand. Then it crashes ninety percent. You hold. 2014. Mount Gox, the biggest exchange in the world, collapses. Hundreds of thousands of coins are gone. 2017. One bitcoin hits twenty thousand dollars. Your dollar is now worth six million. Would you sell? But if you held through every crash, every hack... today, that one dollar is worth tens of millions. The hard part was never buying. It was holding. Follow for more what-ifs.
