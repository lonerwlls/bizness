Monday morning. You open your eyes, and there it is: a green bar floating over your head. Full health. Everyone has one now. And it's going to be so much weirder than you think.
Rule one: everything costs health. You stub your toe on the bed. Minus five. Coffee? Plus ten. Your dad drinks four.
Rule two: you can see who's tired. Your teacher is at twelve percent. On a Monday. Today, nobody asks questions.
Rule three: you can't fake it. Your friend says, "I'm fine." His bar says three. You share your fries. Plus forty.
And the last rule: some things heal you instantly. Your crush laughs at your joke. Your bar fills up and turns gold.
Monday morning. You open your eyes. Full health. Then you stub your toe on the bed. Again.
