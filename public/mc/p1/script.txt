What would happen if Minecraft suddenly became real? Picture this. You wake up on a normal Monday. Your alarm rings, you open your eyes, and something feels wrong. The ceiling is made of squares. Your pillow is a perfect cube. Even the sun outside is a square. You pinch yourself. It hurts. This is not a dream. You run to the window. The whole city has turned into blocks. Trees, roads, cars, clouds, all of it, built from giant cubes, one meter on every side. Cars sit in the road like giant toy bricks. But you are still you. Round head, soft hands, same orange jacket. So is everyone else. Your neighbor stands in the street in his pajamas, poking a square bush. A dog barks at a cube that used to be a fire hydrant. Phones work for about ten minutes. Then the power grid goes completely dark. It happened everywhere, at the exact same second. Eight billion people, one brand new square planet. And very soon, everyone learns the first rule of this world. Gravity doesn't work the way it used to.
