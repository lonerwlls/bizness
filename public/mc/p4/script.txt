Put three planks and two sticks in a simple pattern, and a pickaxe appears instantly. No factory. No workers. No machines. Just a three by three grid and the right recipe. Furniture, tools, armor, even a cake. If you know the pattern, you can make it. Car factories shut down overnight. Why build a car when anyone can craft a rail track in seconds? And then there's your pocket. In this world, you can carry over two thousand blocks at once, and feel nothing. A whole house fits in your backpack. Trucks become pointless. Ships too. Delivery drivers lose their jobs, and moving house takes about five minutes. One person can carry a forest on their back and walk across the country. Builders become the new superstars. A team of ten can raise a castle before dinner. Kids compete to build the tallest tower, and the record changes every hour. Cities start growing upward, made of glass, stone and gold. But the sun is moving faster than it used to. A full day now lasts just twenty minutes. And when the sun goes down, something comes out of the dark.
