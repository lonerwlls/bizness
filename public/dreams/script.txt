Every night, you have the same dream. A red door. A flowerpot. A key underneath. Then one morning, on your way to school, you see it. The red door. You lift the flowerpot. The key is there. Your dreams aren't dreams. They're memories. And it's going to be so much weirder than you think.
Rule one: every place is real. The key opens the red door. Inside, a dusty staircase to the attic. You've never been here. But you know every step.
Rule two: write everything down. That night, you dream of three numbers. Seven. Two. Four. You write them on a sticky note.
Rule three: they're not your memories. In the attic, an old chest with a lock. Seven, two, four. Click. Inside, a photo. A kid in an orange jacket and a beanie with a pompom. On the back: "Grandpa, nineteen sixty-five."
And the last rule: some memories want to be found. Under the photo, there's a map.
Every night, you have the same dream. Tonight, it's an island.
