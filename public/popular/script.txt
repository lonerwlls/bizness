Monday, eight a.m. You walk into school, and the whole hallway turns around. For you. Yesterday, nobody knew your name.
Overnight, you became the most popular kid in school. And it's going to be so much weirder than you think.
Rule one: everyone knows you. Every single person waves. You wave back at all of them. And at one fire extinguisher.
Rule two: your phone never stops. Hearts, likes, invites. Four hundred notifications before lunch. Your thumb needs a vacation.
Rule three: everyone copies you. You wear your hat a little crooked. By Wednesday, the whole school wears your hat. Even the principal.
And the last rule: fame is contagious. Your best friend just stands next to you, and now he's famous too. He signs autographs on napkins.
Next Monday, eight a.m. You walk into school. The whole hallway turns around. And you still wave at the fire extinguisher.
