Day thirteen. You're not the only living thing in the city anymore. Without people, animals move in fast. Pigeons take over the streets. Raccoons raid the trash. Day fourteen, you hear howling. A pack of dogs, left behind by their owners, runs down the avenue. They're hungry, and they're not pets anymore. So you stay high, and you keep your food sealed. Day fifteen, a deer walks down the middle of the highway like it owns the place. Nature comes back faster than we think. Weeds push through the cracks. Birds sing so loud in the morning that they wake you up. Day sixteen, you shoot hoops alone in an empty stadium. Day seventeen, you sit in the best seat of a movie theater, in total darkness, and imagine the film. Day eighteen, it hits you. You haven't seen another human face in eighteen days. So you start drawing faces on the wall of your base, so that someone is always looking back at you.
