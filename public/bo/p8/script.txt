Day thirty. At sunrise, you hear it before you see it. A deep thumping sound, far away. You run to the roof and wave everything you have. The mirror. The beacon. Your arms. And there it is. A helicopter, low over the empty streets, turning toward your SOS. It lands on the roof, and a stranger jumps out and smiles at you. It's the first human face you've seen in a month, and you start crying before you can say a word. They tell you the grid failure was the biggest in history. Engineers have been rebuilding it piece by piece, and your city is next. As the helicopter lifts off, you look down at the place you survived. Your base. The roof fire. The faces on the wall. That night, from the rescue camp, you watch the skyline. And one by one, the lights come back on. So, could you survive thirty days alone in a dark city? What would you grab first?
