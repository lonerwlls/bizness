What if you woke up, and the whole city had gone dark, and you were the only one left in it? It starts with silence. No fridge humming. No traffic. Your alarm never went off, because your phone is dead, and the charger does nothing. You flip the light switch. Nothing. You look out the window, and the street is completely empty. Cars are parked at strange angles, doors still open. A bus sits in the middle of the road with nobody inside. Somewhere far away, a dog is barking, and that's it. You go downstairs and knock on your neighbor's door. No answer. You knock on every door in the building. Nothing. On the corner, a paper notice is flapping on a lamppost. The city was evacuated last night, after the entire power grid failed. And you slept through all of it. This is day one. You have no power, no phone, no internet, and no idea when anyone is coming back. So your plan is simple. Survive thirty days alone.
