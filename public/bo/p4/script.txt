Day eight. You need power, and the city is full of it, if you know where to look. Every parked car has a battery. With a cheap inverter from the hardware store, one car battery can charge your phone again and again. You turn your phone on with shaking hands. No signal. The cell towers had backup batteries, but those died days ago. Day nine, you raid a garden store and grab every solar lamp you can carry. By night, your base glows like a tiny village. Day ten, you build something crazy. A bicycle connected to a small generator. Pedal for an hour, and you get enough power to run a radio. You scan every station. Static. Static. Static. Day eleven, you learn that gas stations are useless. The fuel is still underground, but the pumps need electricity. Day twelve, you cook over a fire in a metal barrel on the roof. Smoke rises over the silent city. And for a moment, you wonder if anyone out there can see it.
