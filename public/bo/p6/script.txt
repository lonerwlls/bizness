Day eleven. You try to find fuel for a generator. But the gas stations are useless. The fuel is still sitting in tanks underground, but the pumps that bring it up need electricity. Day twelve, you start cooking over a fire in a metal barrel on the roof. Smoke rises over the silent city, and for a moment, you wonder if anyone out there can see it. Day thirteen. You realize you're not the only living thing here anymore. Without people, animals move in fast. Pigeons take over the streets. Raccoons tear through the trash. Rats are everywhere at night. Day fourteen, you hear howling. A pack of dogs, left behind by their owners, runs down the avenue. They're hungry, and they're not pets anymore. One of them stops and stares right at you. You back away slowly, and you don't run. Running makes you look like prey. From now on, you stay high, you keep your food sealed in metal boxes, and you never go out after dark. The city isn't yours anymore. You're sharing it.
