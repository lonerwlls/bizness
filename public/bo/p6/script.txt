Day eleven. You try to find fuel for a generator. But the gas stations are useless. The fuel is still sitting in tanks underground, but the pumps that bring it up need electricity. Day twelve, you start cooking over a fire in a metal barrel on the roof. Smoke rises over the silent city, and for a moment, you wonder if anyone out there can see it. Day thirteen. You explore further than ever before, and you go down into the subway. Without pumps, groundwater is slowly flooding the tunnels. Your flashlight shines on black water, already up to the platform. You turn around fast. Day fourteen, you stay out too long, and the sun goes down. Without a single street light, the city turns into a maze. Every corner looks the same. Your flashlight flickers and dies. You stand frozen in the dark, heart pounding, until you look up and find the brightest star, the one you saw from your window. You follow it home, one block at a time. From now on, you have a rule. You are always home before dark.
