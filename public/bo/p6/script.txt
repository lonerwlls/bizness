Day nineteen. Things go wrong. A storm rolls in, and lightning hits a building three blocks away. With no fire trucks and no water pressure, nobody can stop it. You watch the fire jump from roof to roof all night. Day twenty, you cut your hand on broken glass while searching a store. It's deep. In a world with no doctors, a small infection can become dangerous. So you head to the hospital. Its backup generators ran out of fuel long ago. The hallways are pitch black, and every sound echoes. In the pharmacy, you find bandages and antiseptic. You clean the cut, wrap it tight, and hope. Day twenty-two, you get a fever. For two days, you lie on your mattress, drinking rainwater, too weak to stand. You talk to the faces on the wall. On day twenty-four, the fever breaks. You wake up weak, but alive. And you decide something. You're done waiting. It's time to make the world notice you.
