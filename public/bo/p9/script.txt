On day twenty-four, the fever breaks. You wake up weak, but alive. And you decide something. You're done waiting. It's time to make the world notice you. Day twenty-five, you climb to the roof of the tallest building you can reach. Thirty floors of stairs, with no elevator. You stop to rest on every landing. From the top, you can see the whole city. Not a single light. Day twenty-six, you paint a giant SOS on the roof, each letter as big as a car. Pilots are trained to look for signals like that. Day twenty-seven, you collect mirrors from every bathroom in the building. On a sunny day, a mirror flash can be seen from many kilometers away. You spend hours flashing it at the horizon. Day twenty-eight, you carry up car batteries, cables and the brightest lamps you can find, and you build a beacon. When you switch it on that night, it's the only light for kilometers in every direction. A tiny star in a dead city. And you sit next to it until morning.
