There's a bridge at the edge of town, and nobody is allowed to cross it.
It isn't broken, and nobody guards it. There's just one little sign: "Don't wave."
Through the fog on the other side, there's another town, exactly like this one.
Same bakery, same clock tower, same crooked mailbox.
Every morning at seven, the people over there wave at the people over here.
And every morning, nobody waves back.
But Leo has always noticed two weird things.
Their clock runs exactly one minute ahead.
And the guy who waves the hardest wears an orange jacket, just like his.
So one foggy morning, Leo walks to the middle of the bridge... and waves back.
The other guy freezes. Then he smiles, and starts walking toward him.
In the middle, they're face to face: same jacket, same hat, same nervous grin.
Then the fog lifts.
Leo turns around, and the whole town behind him is waving at him.
And the clock on his side of the bridge is now exactly one minute ahead.
