What if every person could see a timer of their life, floating right above their head?
One morning, it just appears. Our guy looks up. Fifty-two years, three months and nine days. Not bad. He smiles.
He steps outside, and the whole street is staring at each other's heads.
The baker has forty years, and keeps baking. The mailman has nine days. He drops his bag and runs straight to the beach.
By lunch, nobody asks "how are you?" anymore. They ask "how long?"
Banks give loans by the timer. Dating apps sort by it.
And our guy? Fifty-two years is plenty. So he waits. Tomorrow. Next month. Next year.
Then one night, he does the math. A third of it, he'll be asleep. Years more, staring at a phone.
That's not fifty-two years. That's maybe twenty.
So he finally gets off the couch.
