Three days later, there's one word everyone is saying. Shortage.
That video of the man buying three bags of rice? It now has forty million views. And suddenly, everyone is buying rice.
The shelves you walked past on Monday are half empty by Thursday morning. Bread is gone by nine. Eggs are gone by ten.
Stores put up new signs: two items per customer. Lines start forming outside before the doors even open.
The café on the corner closes early. Then it doesn't open at all. Without deliveries, there's simply nothing left to cook.
And around the world, it's the same story. Countries that import most of their food are hit first, and they're hit the hardest.
Some governments stop selling food abroad, to protect their own people. Which makes everything even worse for everyone else.
That evening, you come home with half of what you went out for. And for the first time in your life, you open the cupboard... and start counting.
