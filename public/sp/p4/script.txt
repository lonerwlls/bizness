The video goes viral in one hour. Ten million views by dinner. A blurry clip of someone in an orange jacket, flying over the bay and breaking the sound barrier. By morning, news vans are parked outside your house. Reporters shout questions through the door. Drones hover at your window. Your mom's phone won't stop ringing. Then come the serious people. Scientists want blood samples. Generals want to know if you're a weapon. Some countries want you on their side. Others want you gone. Think about it from their side. One person who can fly anywhere, lift anything, read any mind, and can't be hurt. There's no army that can stop you, no prison that can hold you, and no secret you can't hear. To the world, you're not a hero yet. You're the biggest risk on the planet. With telepathy on, you hear all of it. The fear. The plans. The people hoping you're kind. And for the first time you understand that your biggest power isn't flight or strength. It's the choice of what you do next.
