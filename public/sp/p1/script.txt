What if you woke up tomorrow with every superpower at once? Not one power. All of them. Picture this. Your alarm goes off at seven. You reach out to slap it, and the clock explodes into a hundred pieces. Super strength. Okay. Weird. You sit up, but you don't stop. You keep rising, slowly, until your head bumps the ceiling. You're floating. Then the noise hits you. You can hear your neighbor brushing his teeth. A dog barking three streets away. A boat engine far out in the bay. And underneath all of it, voices that nobody is saying out loud. I'm late again. Did I lock the door? You're hearing thoughts. You look down, and the floor turns see-through. Pipes. Wires. A family of mice under the kitchen. X-ray vision. Your heart is beating so fast it sounds like a drum roll, and you realize that's not panic. That's super speed, waiting to be used. You drift to the window. The sun is rising over the water, the little town is still asleep, and nobody knows that the most powerful person on Earth is standing in their socks, trying not to touch anything.
