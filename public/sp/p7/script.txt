Immortality. At first you don't notice it. Then you turn thirty, and you look exactly the same. At fifty, your friends have grey hair and bad knees, and you can still outrun a jet. Your body heals faster than it can age. And that means you will outlive everyone you know. Your mom. Your best friend. The neighbor with the dog. One by one, the faces from the bay disappear, and you keep going. A hundred years pass. The little harbor becomes a city. The lighthouse is replaced by a tower of glass. Two hundred years. Nobody alive remembers the viral video. You're a legend people think was made up. You keep helping, but now you're helping strangers' great great grandchildren. Here's the strange part. Without an ending, nothing feels urgent. There's always tomorrow, and tomorrow, and tomorrow. Scientists say the sun has about five billion years left. You might still be there when it burns out. Floating alone in the dark, with every power in the universe, and no one left to use them for.
