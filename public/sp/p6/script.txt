And then something worse happens. You start to like it. When you can read every mind, people stop surprising you. You know who's lying, who's jealous, who's afraid of you. It's very hard to respect someone when you can hear every ugly thought they've ever had. And power changes people. Psychologists have studied this for decades. In experiments, people given even a little power over others start to notice them less and break more rules. Now imagine having all of it. You could empty any bank vault. Win any election. Freeze time, walk into any room, and take whatever you want, and nobody would ever know. One day a man in a suit offers you a deal. Work for us, and you'll never have to worry about anything again. You read his mind. He isn't afraid of you. He's planning to use you. So here's the real test of every superpower. It's not how strong you are. It's what you refuse to do, even when nobody on Earth can stop you. You fly away without answering. But the hardest power is still waiting.
