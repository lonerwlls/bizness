Every New Year's morning, everyone in town gets a letter from themselves, written one year in the future.
"Don't buy the red car." "Say yes to Anna." "Take an umbrella on the fourth of May."
Nobody ever ignores their letter, and nobody in this town has had a bad year in a hundred years.
But Leo has always noticed two weird things. The postman never leaves footprints in the snow.
And every letter, no matter who it's for, has the exact same loopy capital L.
This year, Leo tears open his envelope... and the page is completely blank.
No warning. No advice. Not a single word.
So that night, he follows the postman's empty trail through the snow, up to the old clock tower.
Inside: a desk, a lamp, a pen, and ten thousand empty envelopes waiting.
And a note, in that same loopy handwriting: "Your turn, Leo. Don't forget the umbrella."
