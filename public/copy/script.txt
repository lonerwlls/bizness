Saturday morning. You walk into the kitchen, and somebody is already eating your cereal. He looks up. It's you. Same jacket. Same hat. Same face. And it's going to be so much weirder than you think.
Rule one: he knows everything you know. You think of a number. He says seven. It was seven.
Rule two: he wants everything you want. One last slice of pizza. You both grab it. You both say "mine." At the same time.
Rule three: nobody can tell you apart. Mom asks who left the socks on the stairs. You point at him. He points at you. Mom takes away both phones.
And the last rule: together, you're unstoppable. Basketball? Two of you. Homework? Half each. Detention? Also half each.
Next Saturday. You walk into the kitchen, and somebody is eating your cereal. You both look up. There are three of you.
