# «Typhoid Mary» — повседневный шортс (≈57 с), стиль «как у Люстига»

N: New York, nineteen oh six. The best cook in town carries a deadly secret.
N: Wherever Mary Mallon cooks, people get typhoid.
N: A banker's summer house. Six of eleven fall sick.
N: An investigator, George Soper, tracks down her old jobs.
SOPER: Seven families. Twenty-two cases. One cook.
SOPER: Miss Mallon, I'll need samples. Of... everything.
MARY: Get out of my kitchen!
N: She chases him out with a carving fork.
N: Then the police come. Mary hides for five hours.
N: The tests are clear. She's full of typhoid germs.
MARY: I've never been sick a day in my life!
N: True. She's a healthy carrier. The first one found in America.
N: The papers call her Typhoid Mary. And they lock her on an island.
N: Three years later, she's freed. On one condition: no more cooking.
N: So she becomes Mary Brown. A cook. At a maternity hospital.
N: Twenty-five people get sick.
N: Back to the island. For twenty-three years. Until she dies.

Голоса (Fish): N — рассказчик канала, SOPER — пожилой мужчина, MARY — женщина с ирландским акцентом.
Факты: Mary Mallon (1869–1938); Oyster Bay 1906 (6 из 11); Джордж Сопер: 7 семей, 22 случая (1900–1907);
март 1907 — полиция и д-р Жозефина Бейкер, Мэри пряталась ~5 часов; остров Норт-Бразер; освобождена в 1910;
1915 — больница Слоун для женщин, 25 заболевших; на острове до смерти в 1938 (23 года).
