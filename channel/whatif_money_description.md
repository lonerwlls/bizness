**Title:**
What If Every Dollar You Spent Cost You One Minute of Your Life?

**Description:**
A $5 coffee costs you 5 minutes of your life. Rent costs you 25 hours. And one day, a bill arrives that isn't even yours.

In this video, money and time become the same thing: every dollar you pay takes one minute off the glowing number above your head. You learn the rules the hard way, start pricing everything in minutes, and slowly discover what that does to your friends, your family — and you.

Real facts in this video:
• Benjamin Franklin wrote "Remember that time is money" in 1748.
• The average new car in the US costs about $48,000 (Kelley Blue Book).
• Harvard researcher Ashley Whillans and her team surveyed over 6,000 people in four countries (2017): people who spent money to save time were more satisfied with their lives.
• The US federal minimum wage is $7.25 an hour — for millions of people, one dollar already costs about 8 minutes of work.
