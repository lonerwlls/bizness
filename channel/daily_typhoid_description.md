**Title:**
The Cook Who Made Half of New York Sick 🍑

**Description:**
1906, New York. Mary Mallon was a great cook, and her peach ice cream was famous. But wherever she worked, people came down with typhoid.
Investigator George Soper traced seven families and twenty-two cases back to one cook. When he asked her for samples, she chased him out of the kitchen with a carving fork.
The tests showed her body was full of typhoid bacteria, yet she had never been sick a day. She was a "healthy carrier", the first one ever identified in the United States.
She was isolated on North Brother Island, released in 1910 on the promise never to cook again… and then took a job as "Mary Brown", a cook at a maternity hospital. Twenty-five people got sick.
She spent the next 23 years on the island, until her death in 1938.
