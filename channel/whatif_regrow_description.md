**Title:**
What If You Could Regrow Any Body Part?

**Description:**
3 a.m., a hospital break room. You're slicing a bagel, the knife slips — and forty seconds later your fingertip has grown back. Nail and all.

In this video, Doctor ROO can regrow anything: skin, teeth, a slice of liver, even a kidney. He becomes "the Spare Parts Doctor", turns down a billion dollars — and then his blood test shows what fast growth really costs. And when a seven-year-old named Lily needs a liver, he has to decide whether to roll the dice one more time.

Real facts in this video:
• Axolotls can regrow legs, parts of the heart and even pieces of the brain — without scars.
• In 1898 Thomas Hunt Morgan showed a piece of a flatworm about 1/279 of its size can grow into a whole new worm.
• In 1974 Dr. Cynthia Illingworth (Sheffield) reported that children's cut-off fingertips often grow back if they're left to heal instead of being stitched closed.
• In 2024 doctors in Kyoto began the first human trial of a drug meant to help people grow new teeth.
• A living donor's liver grows back to almost full size in about two months.
• In 2006 Dr. Anthony Atala reported seven young patients living with bladders grown from their own cells; in 2022 a young woman received an ear 3D-printed from her own cells.
• Deer antlers can grow up to about 2 cm a day — and in 2019 scientists found their growth genes look a lot like bone-cancer genes.
• In 1986 Dr. Harold Dvorak described tumors as "wounds that do not heal".
• In 2022 Michael Levin's team at Tufts University put a dome with five drugs on a frog's leg for 24 hours — over 18 months, the frog regrew a working leg.
