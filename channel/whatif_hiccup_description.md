**Title:**
What If You Hiccuped for 68 Years?

**Description:**
You're 95, on a late-night TV show, and the host asks one question: how many times have you hiccuped? Over four hundred million. And counting.

In this video, Doctor ROO catches a falling patient on a night shift, and something inside him goes "pop". The hiccups never stop. He has to give up surgery, blend every meal, and listen to every heartbeat between his own hiccups. Then, after sixty-eight years, they stop.

This story is based on a real person: Charles Osborne, an American farmer who hiccuped from 1922 to 1990.

Real facts in this video:
• In 1922 Charles Osborne lifted a 350-pound hog, fell, and began to hiccup. He didn't stop for 68 years.
• His doctor thought a tiny blood vessel had burst in the part of the brain that controls hiccups.
• He hiccuped about 20 to 40 times a minute. Over his life that added up to roughly 430 million hiccups.
• His longest break was about 50 hours.
• One theory says hiccups are left over from tadpoles, which close their throats like that when they breathe through gills. Babies even hiccup in the womb.
• In 2021 scientists tested a special drinking straw. 92% of users said it stopped their hiccups.
• Osborne got thousands of letters with "cures". A friend even fired a shotgun behind his back. Nothing worked.
• He married twice, raised eight children, and is in Guinness World Records for the longest attack of hiccups. They stopped in 1990, and he died a year later at 97.
