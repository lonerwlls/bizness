**Title:**
The Town Where You Can't Die ❄️

**Description:**
Longyearbyen, Svalbard — about 2,400 people, deep inside the Arctic.
The ground here is permafrost: it never thaws. Bodies buried here simply don't decay, so back in the 1950s the tiny cemetery stopped taking new burials.
In 1998 scientists even dug up victims of the 1918 flu to look for traces of the virus.

So if you get seriously ill, you're flown to mainland Norway. Moms-to-be leave weeks before their due date — there's no maternity ward.
It's not actually a law against dying… but nobody here plans to stay forever.
