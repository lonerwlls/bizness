**Title:**
He Drank Bacteria to Prove Doctors Wrong 🧪

**Description:**
1984, Australia. Dr. Barry Marshall drank a glass full of bacteria — on purpose.
Back then, every doctor "knew" stomach ulcers came from stress and spicy food. Marshall and his colleague Robin Warren blamed a tiny spiral bacterium called Helicobacter pylori. The experts laughed. So he became his own experiment: three days later he was throwing up, and a camera in his stomach showed it inflamed and full of the bacteria. Antibiotics fixed it.
Today most ulcers are cured in about two weeks — and in 2005, Marshall and Warren won the Nobel Prize.
