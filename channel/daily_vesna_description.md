# Шортс «Весна Вулович» — для YouTube

**Title:**
She Fell 10 km Without a Parachute… and Lived ✈️

**Description:**
January 26, 1972. A bomb tears a passenger jet apart 33,000 feet above Czechoslovakia.
Everyone on board dies — except one flight attendant, Vesna Vulović, who wasn't even supposed to be on that flight.

Pinned inside a piece of the plane by a food cart, she fell 10,160 metres, hit a snowy, wooded mountainside at just the right angle, and was found alive by a villager who had been a medic in the war.
Fractured skull, broken legs, three broken vertebrae — and ten months later she was walking again.
It's still the Guinness World Record for the highest fall survived without a parachute.
